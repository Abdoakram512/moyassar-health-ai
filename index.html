The Core Advantage • Anthropic Claude 3.7 Integration

What Claude Does: The Clinical Reasoning Core

Machine learning models and computer vision only detect bounding boxes and numerical biomarkers. Anthropic Claude 3.7 Sonnet serves as the clinical brain, connecting diagnostic outputs with medical logic to generate actionable clinician notes and empathetic patient explanations.

1

Multi-Modal Cross-Specialty Synthesis

Claude correlates disparate findings—such as blood CBC anemia markers and dental oral mucosa inflammation—to identify systemic root causes.

2

Extended Clinical Thinking & Differential Diagnostics

Utilizing Claude 3.7's extended reasoning tokens, the model evaluates subtle differential diagnoses (e.g., Nutritional Iron Deficiency vs. Beta-Thalassemia Trait) before finalizing recommendations.

3

Standardized Physician SOAP Notes

Converts raw ML confidence vectors into standardized Subjective, Objective, Assessment, and Plan (SOAP) clinical documentation with ICD-10 diagnostic coding.

4

Empathetic Patient Explanations (Arabic & English)

Demystifies intimidating medical jargon into compassionate, clear health guidance tailored for patients in both Arabic and English.

Clinical Architecture Pipeline

آلية العمل المتكاملة • End-to-End Mechanism of Work

How raw patient imagery and laboratory panels transform into verified clinical intelligence across 4 coordinated stages.

LAYER 1: PERCEPTION

Computer Vision & Deep Learning

Models: YOLOv8, ResNet-50, TabNet, OpenCV, CNNs.
Role: Raw sensory perception. Detects bounding boxes around dental caries, measures blood cell morphology, and segments brain MRI slices. Outputs numerical probabilities without clinical medical context.

LAYER 2: CLINICAL REASONING

Anthropic Claude 3.7 Sonnet Core

Model: Claude 3.7 Sonnet with Extended Reasoning Tokens.
Role: The Clinical Medical Brain. Synthesizes multi-specialty findings, performs differential diagnosis deliberation, checks contraindications, drafts standardized physician SOAP notes (with ICD-10 codes), and produces empathetic patient explanations in Arabic and English.

Phase 1

Diagnostic Ingestion

Blood lab panels, dental radiographs, or brain MRIs are securely submitted via TLS 1.3 encryption without storing patient identity (zero persistent PII).

Specialized Vision / ML
Phase 2

Feature Extraction

YOLOv8 & ResNet localize dental lesions (0.78 mAP), TabNet calculates CBC cell distributions, and CNN models segment MRI tumors.

Phase 3

Claude 3.7 Reasoning

Anthropic Claude 3.7's clinical reasoning core correlates cross-specialty findings, performs extended clinical thinking, and formats standardized SOAP notes.

Anthropic Claude 3.7 API
Phase 4

Dual-Audience Delivery

Physicians receive structured clinical documentation with ICD-10 coding, while patients receive an empathetic, plain-language summary in Arabic and English.
